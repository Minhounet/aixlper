# Acme team skills

Shared AI skills for the billing teams (Java 21, Maven, JUnit 5, Spring Boot).

## Format
- One skill per folder: `skills/<name>/SKILL.md`, frontmatter `name` and
  `description` only.
- Names say what the skill does, in kebab-case (`java-testing`, not a
  nickname).
- English, short imperative sentences. A skill must not depend on a skill
  outside this repository.

## Review
Every new skill or change goes through a merge request reviewed by two
members of the platform team.
